# Azokle Weather v1.1 Changelog

Welcome to Azokle Weather v1.1! This update brings a complete visual redesign, ultra-smooth fluid graphs, massive performance optimizations, and a refreshed app logo.

---

## What's New

### 🎨 Modern Claymorphism Design
- **Tactile Weather Cards**: Redesigned all weather cards and tiles with soft clay surfaces, gentle highlights, and subtle depth contours.
- **Theme-Adaptive Palettes**: Carefully balanced light and dark mode colors with high contrast and readability.
- **Phosphor Icons**: Replaced default vectors with clean, modern Phosphor icons across all navigation, search, and settings screens.

### 📈 Smooth & Fluid Weather Graphs
- **Silky Temperature Curves**: Upgraded hourly temperature lines from jagged segments to smooth, natural cubic curves with soft gradient glows.
- **Floating Pill Badges**: Added tactile floating badges for daily minimum and maximum temperatures and peak rain chance.
- **Rounded Precipitation Bars**: Redesigned rain, showers, and snow volume charts with rounded capsule bars.
- **Capsule Date Selector**: New horizontal scrollable date bar with tactile clay pills to switch between forecast days.

### ⚡ Blazing Fast Performance & Animations
- **Zero-Lag Scrolling**: Optimized card rendering with hardware-accelerated drawing and GPU caching for steady 120fps scrolling.
- **Instant Search Bar**: Fixed search bar delays so opening, typing, and exiting the search screen feels immediate and responsive.
- **Smooth Loading Animations**: Loading skeletons now animate directly on the GPU without freezing or stuttering the UI.

### 📱 New App Logo & Launcher Icons
- Updated the official Azokle Weather logo with full adaptive icon support across all modern Android devices.
